Referring to our specialists

We accept referrals from practitioners locally, across Australia and internationally, and work with referring clinicians to meet their requirements and those of their patients.

Referral formCall main rooms

How to refer

  1. Choose a specialist

    Address the referral to one of our doctors, or to the practice if you'd like us to match your patient.

  2. Send the referral

    Use the referral form, or contact our main rooms in Frankston on 03 9070 6116.

  3. We arrange the visit

    Our team arranges a time and clinic with your patient, at whichever of our seven locations suits.
